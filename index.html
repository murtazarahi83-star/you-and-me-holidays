Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about planning your next holiday with us.

Which destinations can I enquire about?

You can enquire about holidays to destinations around the world. Tell us where you would like to travel and our team can help you explore suitable options.

Can I request flights without a hotel?

Yes. Select Flights in our enquiry form and provide your departure airport, destination, travel dates and passenger details.

Can I enquire about hotels only?

Yes. Select Hotels and share your destination, check-in and check-out dates, number of guests and room preferences.

Can I request a personalised holiday package?

Yes. Tell us your preferred destination, travel dates, number of travellers, budget and holiday preferences so we can explore suitable options.

Can I review my enquiry before sending it?

Yes. You can review your details and make changes before choosing to continue through WhatsApp or Email.

Is my enquiry submitted automatically?

No. Your enquiry details will be prepared in WhatsApp or your email app. You must press Send to submit your message to our team.
