Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information about contacting our travel team.

Which destinations can I enquire about?

You can enquire about destinations worldwide. Tell us where you would like to travel.

Can I request flights only?

Yes. Choose Flights in the enquiry form and provide your departure airport, destination and dates.

Can I request a hotel only?

Yes. Choose Hotels and provide your destination, check-in and check-out dates, guests and room requirements.

Can I review my enquiry before sending?

Yes. You can review all your details and edit them before choosing WhatsApp or Email.

Will my enquiry be sent automatically?

No. Your message will be prepared in WhatsApp or your email app. You must press Send to submit it to our team.
