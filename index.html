Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information about contacting our travel team.

Which destinations can I enquire about?

You can contact us about destinations worldwide. Tell us where you would like to travel and we can discuss your requirements.

Can I request flights only?

Yes. Select Flights Only in the enquiry form and provide your departure airport, destination and travel dates.

Can I request a luxury or honeymoon holiday?

Yes. Choose the relevant holiday type and tell us about your preferred accommodation, experiences and budget.

How do I send a holiday enquiry?

Complete the enquiry form and select Send Enquiry on WhatsApp. Review the prepared message and press Send in WhatsApp.

Can I change my travel requirements later?

You can contact our team to discuss changes to your travel preferences and requirements.
